# Much More Dungeons 2.0.1

## Fixed

- **Dungeon spawners now spawn mobs.** The torches, lanterns and other lights in the dungeon rooms lit every block
  around most spawners, and Overworld monsters only spawn from a spawner in complete darkness. 36 of the 45
  spawners (labyrinth spawner rooms, pillar halls and vaults, the Tier 2 dungeon, the Tower Dungeon and the
  Soulfire Crypt) could never spawn anything. Every dungeon spawner now spawns its mobs whatever the light level,
  so the rooms keep their lighting. Placing torches no longer switches these spawners off; break them instead.

Applies to dungeons generated after updating. Spawners in dungeons that already generated keep their old
behaviour.
